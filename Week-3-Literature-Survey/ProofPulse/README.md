# ProofPulse – Literature Survey

## Proposed Project

**ProofPulse: Stateful Action-Level Dynamic Trust Verification**

## Objective

ProofPulse is proposed as a cloud-deployable Zero Trust security system that continuously evaluates user, device, behavioral and contextual signals during an active session.

Instead of relying only on authentication at login, the system evaluates security-relevant actions and dynamically determines whether the next action should be allowed, require stronger verification, or be blocked.

## Research Direction

The proposed system will integrate Zero Trust, continuous authentication, behavioral analysis, dynamic trust and attribute-based authorization.

The proposed research direction is to investigate whether sequences of security-sensitive actions can be represented as trust-state transitions that influence the assurance requirement for subsequent actions.

The final novelty and patentability of the proposed contribution will require separate prior-art and patent analysis.
