# Paper 5 — Anomaly Detection and Root-Cause Identification in Microservices: A Survey

**Project:** TesseraGuard — Aggregation-Aware API Threat Detection & Enforcement Gateway

**Source:** Cluster Computing, Springer, 2026

**Paper:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10586-026-06095-9

## What the Existing Solution Is

A comprehensive survey of **117 studies** on microservice anomaly detection from 2012–2025.

The studies are categorized by:

* Data collection method
* Detection algorithm
* Diagnostic model
* Evaluation metric

The survey maps the full landscape of the field.

## Which Part Is Used in the Proposed Solution

Not a technique to adopt directly, but a **landscape-verification tool**.

It is used to confirm where TesseraGuard's specific contribution — combination-based risk scoring — sits relative to all 117 reviewed studies.

It can also surface any additional directly relevant work not yet found.

## How It Is Useful

Before finalizing the novelty claim, this survey provides a way to check whether **"aggregation-based combination risk scoring"** already exists as a named, solved category among the reviewed studies.

It also serves as a citation index for the final report.
