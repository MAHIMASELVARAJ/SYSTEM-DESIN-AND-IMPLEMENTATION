**Source:** ScienceDirect, January 2026

**Paper:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/org/science/article/pii/S1546221826000500

## What the Existing Solution Is

The most current available literature review of ICS/OT intrusion detection research, covering **January 2017–August 2025**.

It maps:

* Technical approaches
* Machine learning adoption trends
* Edge computing adoption trends
* Open challenges in the field

## Which Part Is Used in the Proposed Solution

Not a technique to adopt, but a **landscape-verification tool**.

It is used to confirm where ReflexGuard's specific contribution — **automatic edge-level network isolation as detection's direct output** — sits relative to the full body of reviewed ICS/OT research.

## How It Is Useful

Before finalizing the novelty claim, this is the fastest way to check whether **"automatic edge-level isolation, not just alerting"** already exists as a solved, named category anywhere in the 2017–2025 ICS/OT literature.

The full text should be read directly to confirm this.
